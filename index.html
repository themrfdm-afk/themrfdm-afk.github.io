The short version

I started writing about sports at 16 for my hometown paper, and I haven't really stopped. Since then I've picked up a camera, a podcast mic and a deadline-friendly sense of calm, and I now cover Nebraska athletics as a photographer and reporter for The Daily Nebraskan.

What I love most is the people behind the scoreboard: the coaches, the walk-ons, the players nobody's writing about yet. Whether it's a feature, a photo or a postgame interview, I want you to feel like you were there.

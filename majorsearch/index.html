UNL Majors: Who’s Studying What

Search more than 300 majors and programs at the University of Nebraska–Lincoln and see how many students have been enrolled in each every fall since 2016.

Counts are students enrolled with that major listed as their first major in the fall term, by college and degree. A student with two majors is counted under each. Programs are matched across years by name and by university major code, so a program that was renamed or moved colleges generally appears as one line, but some small or short-lived programs may be split. Counts for filtered views include only the selected college or degree level.

Source: UNL Institutional Research and Planning, “Majors by College” (DR12573), Fall 2016–Fall 2025.

Groundhog Day Predictors

Search 93 weather-predicting critters across the U.S. and Canada by name, location or kind of creature, and see how each has called Groundhog Day since 1990.

A shadow means six more weeks of winter; no shadow means an early spring. The data records what each animal predicted, not whether the weather followed through, so “track record” here is each predictor’s history of calls. Data: groundhog-day.com via The Art of Data Journalism.

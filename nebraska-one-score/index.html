How to play

×

Nebraska lost a game by one score (8 points or fewer) sometime between 2015 and 2026. Find it in 6 guesses. Then play again, as many times as you want.

  • Pick any one-score loss from the list. The tiles show how close you are.
  • Green means exact.
  • Yellow means close (within 2 years, 2 points of margin, or 3 Husker points).
  • Gray means not close. Arrows point toward the answer.
  • Each guess unlocks a new clue from the box score. Each clue tells you which tile it helps with.
  • Crack it and you go up a win. Run out of guesses (or give up) and it's a loss. Your record and streak are saved on this computer.
